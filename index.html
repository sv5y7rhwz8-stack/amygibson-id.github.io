Module 02

Teaching & Design Philosophy

What guides my decisions when I design a course or a lesson.

Communication and collaboration are the most important elements for building real community in a learning space, whether it happens online or in person.
01

Video feedback

Courses are built around feedback delivered on video, not just in the margins of a document. It's harder to misread tone that way.

02

Collaborative work

Assignments are designed so students build things together instead of just submitting work side by side.

03

Facilitated discussion

Discussion is structured and guided, not left to run on its own. A facilitator keeps it purposeful.

04

Unpolished, on purpose

Instructional videos stay minimally edited and personal. A familiar voice teaches better than a slick production.

Module 03

About

From flight line to classroom.

I'm an educator with eight years of classroom experience, currently teaching at CrossPointe Preparatory, a University-Model® school in Searcy, Arkansas. Before that, I served as a UH-60 Crew Chief in the U.S. Army, coordinating aircraft maintenance and mission readiness and mentoring junior personnel. That environment taught me a lot about training people when the stakes are real. I carried it into education: I hold a B.S. in Psychology from Upper Iowa University and am now finishing an M.Ed. in Instructional Design at Quinnipiac University.

Day to day, my work spans curriculum development, building courses in Canvas, developing eLearning with tools like Articulate 360 and Storyline, and training other teachers to use these tools well. I also work remotely as a Scoring Assistant for Pearson Education.
