01

Herbal Identification & First Aid

  • Canvas module
  • Canvas LMS
  • HTML/CSS
  • Video
  • Quinnipiac IDN 532

A Canvas module that teaches learners to identify common medicinal plants and use them safely for basic first aid. I designed and built every page, the media, and the activities from the ground up.

  • A narrated microlecture on the history of medicinal plants, plus an herbal infographic, to build context
  • An interactive field guide for comparing elderberry, dandelion, and echinacea, followed by a low-stakes formative check with feedback
  • A peer identification discussion, and a hands-on elderberry syrup project as the final product

About

I teach at CrossPointe Preparatory in Searcy, Arkansas, and I'm completing an M.Ed. in Instructional Design at Quinnipiac University. My work spans curriculum development, Canvas course building, eLearning in Articulate 360 and Storyline, and training other teachers to use these tools well.

Before the classroom, I served in the U.S. Army as a UH-60 Crew Chief, where I learned what it takes to train people when the stakes are real. I also hold a B.S. in Psychology from Upper Iowa University, and I've been an online learner myself since the early 2000s.

Communication and collaboration are what turn a course into a learning community.
